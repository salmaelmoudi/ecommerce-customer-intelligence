🚚  Operations & Delivery
Key Insight: Total revenue of R$ 15,665,892 generated across 99,441 orders. Revenue grew sharply through 2017, peaking in Nov 2017 (Black Friday effect). Credit card accounts for >74% of payments. Top 3 categories (bed_bath_table, health_beauty, sports_leisure) generate over 35% of total revenue. Overall on-time delivery rate: 92.1%.
Key Insight: Of 94,697 unique customers, over 90% are one-time buyers — retention is the single biggest growth lever. Champions (top RFM segment, 15,471 customers) have an average lifetime value 8× higher than Lost Customers. São Paulo (SP) drives ~38% of total revenue.
Key Insight: Late deliveries statistically and significantly reduce customer satisfaction: avg review 4.294 (on-time) vs 2.566 (late), Mann-Whitney p ≈ 0.0. A Logistic Regression model predicts delays at order time (ROC-AUC = 0.6748). Top predictor: estimated delivery days — the longer the promised window, the higher the actual delay risk. Average delivery time: 12.0 days.
